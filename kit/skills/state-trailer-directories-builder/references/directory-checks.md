Checked: 2026-11-17

Method: each directory's own add-business or sign-up page was opened read-only in a browser. No form was filled or submitted. A page behind a Cloudflare check was not bypassed and is marked "blocked, not read". Source lists: BrightLocal "Ultimate USA free business directory list" and 1Solutions "Top 100 free business listing directories in the USA".

Result: none of the 20 below is confirmed as "free, no account, no email". The closest is ThreeBestRated (no account, but it asks for the business email, photos and a CAPTCHA, and it selects only top businesses). Directories almost never list by email alone.
